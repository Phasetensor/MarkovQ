## Regression coverage and reference independence

The complete suite includes all original classical and quantum tests. It exercises row transitions and chronological matrix products, SCC classes, return generators, first passage, absorption, stationarity, mixing, CTMC semigroups, selected Lévy increment laws, graph spectra, unitary actions, monitored renewal and open-system physicality. The added suites are tests/test_numerical_stress.py and tests/test_consolidation_regressions.py. Analytically specified kernels with absorption probabilities down to 2⁻⁴⁰ supplement the prior reducible and periodic cases. Property-style cases use fixed random seeds for graph weights, initial states and changes of basis. All original statistical experiments retain their seed and uncertainty convention.

The Decimal references evaluate small analytical models independently of SciPy's Schur or Lyapunov routines. The finite spectral kernel and direct step-by-step iteration are distinct algorithms. Szegedy checks include componentwise row/column reflections independent of sparse isometry assembly. Lindblad checks include analytically solved populations, the direct energy-basis dissipative reference, dense vectorized generators and QuTiP. The Qiskit circuit uses elementary gates independently of MarkovQ's coined operator. Tests that check method agreement alone cannot certify the underlying physical model.
